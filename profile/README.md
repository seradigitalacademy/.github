# Sera Digital Marketing Academy

**Digital marketing you can put to work.**

Sera Digital Marketing Academy is a practitioner-led digital marketing academy in Navrangpura, Ahmedabad. Our comprehensive 16-week Digital Marketing Course helps beginners, fresh graduates and career switchers learn to plan, run, measure and explain real marketing work.

## The course
- 16 weeks, 48 live two-hour sessions (96 live hours), plus a weekly session for practice, feedback and doubts
- Modules: Marketing Foundations, Content Marketing, SEO and AI Search, Performance Marketing, Social Media, Analytics, and an integrated capstone
- In our Ahmedabad classroom or live online, with evening batch options
- You leave with a portfolio of work you can show and explain
